COMMERCIAL OFFICE LEASE

This Commercial Office Lease is made as of May 1, 2017 by and between
Harbor Point Properties LLC (the "Landlord"), a Delaware limited
liability company, and Coastal Devices Inc. (the "Tenant"), a Delaware
corporation.

1. Premises. Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant leases from Landlord,
Suite 400 of the building located at 1200 Harbor Point Way, Wilmington,
Delaware, consisting of approximately 14,200 rentable square feet.

2. Term and Rent. The initial term is seven years commencing July 1,
2017. Base rent is $31.50 per rentable square foot per year, payable in
equal monthly installments and subject to annual escalation of two and
one-half percent.

3. Use. The premises shall be used for general office, engineering, and
laboratory purposes and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior
written consent.

4. Assignment and Subletting. Tenant shall not assign this Lease or
sublet all or any portion of the premises without Landlord's prior
written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Any merger,
consolidation, sale of substantially all assets, or other change in
control of Tenant shall be deemed an assignment for purposes of this
Section and shall require Landlord's prior written consent on the same
basis.

5. Default. Failure to pay rent within five days after written notice,
or breach of any other covenant not cured within thirty days after
written notice, constitutes an event of default.

6. Governing Law. This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State
of Delaware.
