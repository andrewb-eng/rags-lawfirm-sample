EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Executive Employment Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into
as of August 15, 2018 by and between Coastal Devices Inc. (the
"Company"), a Delaware corporation, and Anita Desai (the "Executive").

1. Position and Duties. The Company employs the Executive as Chief
Operating Officer. The Executive shall devote substantially all business
time to the affairs of the Company and shall report to the Chief
Executive Officer.

2. Compensation. The Executive shall receive an annual base salary of
$360,000, reviewed annually, and shall be eligible for an annual target
bonus equal to forty-five percent of base salary and for equity awards
under the Company's incentive plans.

3. Change in Control Severance. If, within eighteen months following a
change in control of the Company — including any merger or consolidation
after which the holders of the Company's voting securities immediately
prior thereto hold less than fifty percent of the voting power of the
surviving entity — the Executive's employment is terminated by the
Company without Cause or the Executive resigns for Good Reason, then the
Executive shall receive continued payment of base salary for twelve
months, one hundred percent of the target bonus for the year of
termination, reimbursement of COBRA premiums for up to twelve months,
and accelerated vesting of one hundred percent of the Executive's
then-unvested equity awards, subject to execution of a release of
claims.

4. At-Will Employment. The Executive's employment is at will and may be
terminated by either party at any time, subject to Section 3.

5. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the
State of Delaware.
